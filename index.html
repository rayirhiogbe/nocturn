Questions, answered

How do the tees fit?

Boxy and slightly cropped. Size up for an oversized drape, or keep your usual size for a clean fit.

How long does shipping take?

Orders leave within 2 business days. Delivery takes 3 to 7 days, and shipping is free over $100.

Can I return an order?

Yes. Send any unworn item back within 30 days and we refund it to your original payment method.

Are the prints durable?

Prints are cured at high heat and stay sharp after repeat washes. Wash cold and inside out for the longest life.

Do you restock sold out pieces?

Most runs are limited. Join the drop list in the footer and we email you before anything returns.
